# Comparative product notes (official sources)

- Quran.com: study mode brings tafsir, word details, bookmarks and related material beside an ayah; useful pattern for contextual reading. https://quran.com/en/product-updates/new-study-mode-on-quran-com
- Bayyinah TV: clear routes by surah, subject and learner stage, with resume/progress. https://bayyinah.tv/
- Tarteel: goal tracking, recitation support, bookmarks and typography controls. https://support.tarteel.ai/en/collections/15105266-tarteel-features
- Muslim Pro: broad utility bundle, but Noor al-Ilm concentrates on transparent source-led learning. https://www.muslimpro.com/features/
- Sunnah.com: clear collection/book/bab reference patterns and an API with manual checks. https://sunnah.com/developers
- Quran Foundation API: content sync and peer-reviewed translation rules; permission-dependent implementation. https://api-docs.quran.com/docs/tutorials/faq/
- QuranEnc: translation source candidate requiring per-edition licensing review. https://quranenc.com/en/home/api/

These are interaction benchmarks, not copied branding, content or assets. A detailed legal/UX audit of each product remains pending.

# Commercial, Legal, and Growth Plan

**Purpose:** prevent premature commercial claims. **Status:** proposed; payments and partner rewards remain disabled.

## Packaging

- **Free:** approved foundational learning and provenance access, subject to source rights.
- **Premium (hypothesis):** enhanced study plans, family tools, saved visual packs, and institutional reporting must not remove access to core religious knowledge.
- **Institutional (future):** organization workspace, controlled cohorts, export, and SLA after tenancy/security delivery.

The draft Egypt prices (EGP 79 monthly / EGP 699 annually, 90-day trial) are hypotheses only. Pricing, payment-provider availability, taxes, invoices, refunds, and merchant eligibility require dated official verification and legal/accounting approval.

## Legal/document requirements before public launch

| Area | Needed evidence | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Privacy and terms | counsel-reviewed locale-aware policies, data controller/contact | legal owner |
| Children | age design, guardian consent, retention/deletion, escalation | safeguarding owner |
| Copyright/licensing | Quran/tafsir/hadith/translation/image/font permissions | content/rights owner |
| Payments | provider contract, webhook security, refund/cancel lifecycle | finance/engineering |
| Partner rewards | signed consent, inventory, fraud/refund policy | partnerships owner |

## Analytics events

Proposed events: `world_opened`, `source_viewed`, `approved_lesson_completed`, `question_served`, `question_answered`, `correction_created`, `review_decision_recorded`, `release_published`, `support_requested`. No content body, answer text, or unnecessary personal data belongs in event payloads.

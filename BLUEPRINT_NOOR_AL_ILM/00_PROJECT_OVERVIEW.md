# Project Overview

**Purpose:** establish the product thesis and scope. **Status:** mixed current/pilot and proposed target. **Sources:** existing code, Product Requirements, Editorial Policy.

## Product thesis

NOOR AL-ILM is an Arabic-first global Islamic education platform that makes a learner’s path, a claim’s source, and a content item’s review state visible. The immediate problem is not a lack of isolated information: it is difficulty distinguishing trustworthy study material from unverified claims and engaging learners without hiding provenance.

**Primary users:** curious beginners, independent learners, families, researchers, editors/reviewers, and eventually institutions. **Buyer/operator distinction:** individuals learn; a future institution licenses administration and reporting; reviewers decide whether a claim advances in the editorial process.

## Market position

The platform differentiates by explicit provenance, review status, versioned corrections, and Arabic typographic visual learning. It must never compete through unsourced religious answers or invented endorsements. Direct/indirect alternatives and public pricing require a dedicated, dated market-research pass before commercial positioning is finalized.

## Success metrics (proposed)

- **North Star:** weekly verified learning actions: completed approved lesson or explained approved question.
- Activation: a signed-in learner finishes one approved learning unit and views its source.
- Quality: percentage of published items with source, rights record, scholar review, and correction path.
- Trust: correction acknowledgement and resolution time; rejected/withdrawn published items.
- Safety: zero known unauthorized access to draft source text or child data.

No numerical targets are asserted until baseline instrumentation and an approved launch cohort exist.

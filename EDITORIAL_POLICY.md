# Editorial policy

No raw archive paragraph is published as religious guidance. Verify Quran text against a versioned canonical source with surah/ayah, distinguish translation of meanings from Arabic scripture and exegesis, check hadith wording/collection/number/grade and divergent grades, and attribute historical statements to precise sources. Do not create legal opinions or mark a generated answer as a fatwa.

A researcher records references; a fact-checker checks wording; a language editor checks clarity; a qualified scholar reviews religious meaning; a separate publisher authorizes release. Corrections remain versioned and visible. The `/api/admin/review` endpoint records an authenticated allowlisted review and audit event, but never publishes automatically. Allowlist configuration and role-specific checks must be extended before production editorial use.

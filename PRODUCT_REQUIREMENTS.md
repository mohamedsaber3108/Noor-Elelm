# Product requirements

Core promise: a welcoming, visual, Arabic-first library of Islamic learning with visible source and review state. Information hierarchy: World → Topic → Chapter → Lesson → Claim → Infographic/Question. Audience: beginners, individuals, families, researchers and eventually institutional editors. Basic knowledge stays free, subject to source rights.

Pilot: 16 worlds, source-heading search, Quran reference hub, mosque discovery placeholders, infographic prototype, internal quiz and immutable-once-per-question trial ledger, signed-in dashboard, methodology and review record API. No unverified source bodies appear as lessons.

Future gates: human scholarly team and approvals; licensed Quran and hadith integration; translation workflow for 10 languages; full lesson CMS and thematic search; child account consent; real rewards and partner approvals; checkout and tax review; accessible and load-tested public launch.
